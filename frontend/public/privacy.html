REBU Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

REBU ("REBU", "we", "us") runs the REBU app, a live video and voice calling app where users talk with verified hosts. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the REBU Android app and the services at rebu.live.

REBU is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

1. Information we collect

Account information. Your mobile number (used to sign in with a one-time code), or your email address and password if you sign up with email. Your name, gender, date of birth, profile photos, bio, city and languages that you add to your profile. Your age is shown on your profile; your date of birth is not. Your phone number is never shown to other users.

Host verification (hosts only). If you apply to become a host: a selfie taken in the app, and a photo and number of a government ID (such as Aadhaar, PAN, passport or driving licence). These are used only to confirm your identity and age and are seen only by our verification team, never by other users. To receive payouts, hosts also give us bank account or UPI details.

Activity in the app. Messages you send, gifts you send and receive, follows, the calls you make or receive (who, when and how long, for billing), live streams and audio rooms you start or join, posts ("moments") and comments, reports and blocks, and profile visits.

Calls and live streams. Video and audio are transmitted in real time through our calling provider. We do not record calls or live streams.

Purchases and earnings. Coin purchases, coin balance, and for hosts their earnings and withdrawals. Payments are handled by Razorpay; we receive the payment status and reference, never your full card or bank login details.

Device information. Your device platform and app version, and a notification token so we can send you notifications.

Camera and microphone. The app asks for camera and microphone access for video and voice calls, live streams, and the selfie and ID photos described above. We do not access your location, contacts or files.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To show your profile and connect calls, chats, gifts, lives and rooms.
  • To charge for calls, messages and gifts, sell coins, and pay hosts their earnings.
  • To verify hosts' identity and age, and to approve or reject host applications.
  • To keep REBU safe: review reports, moderate content, and suspend or ban accounts that break our rules.
  • To send you important notices and, where you allow it, notifications about activity and offers.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations and to resolve disputes.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show third-party advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • Other users see your public profile (name, age, photos, bio, city, languages, level and badges) and what you post or send to them. They never see your phone number, email, date of birth, selfie or ID.
  • Service providers who run parts of REBU for us, only to the extent needed: cloud hosting and database (our servers and MongoDB Atlas), real-time calling (Agora), payments (Razorpay), SMS for sign-in codes, and push notifications (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Agencies: if a host joins through a talent agency, that agency receives a share of the host's earnings, and we may tell the agency the earnings totals its share is based on.
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect the safety of our users or others.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your name, photos, bio, email and phone number from your profile straight away. We keep payment, coin and earnings records for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Messages you already sent may stay visible to the people you sent them to, shown as coming from "Deleted user". Host verification documents are kept while needed for legal and fraud-prevention purposes and are deleted on request (see section 6).

5. Security

Data is sent to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to verification documents and admin tools is limited to authorised staff. Screenshots and screen recording are blocked inside calls, chats, live rooms and the wallet. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile at any time in the app.
  • Hide your distance and online status, or use incognito mode, in Settings.
  • Block and report anyone from their profile, a chat or a call.
  • Delete your account in the app: Me → Settings → Delete account. You can also ask us to delete it, including any host verification documents, from rebu.live/delete-account.html.
  • Access or correct your information by writing to us.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant we will tell you in the app. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.

8. Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy: support@rebu.app
