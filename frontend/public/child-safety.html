REBU Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · REBU · rebu.live

REBU has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits or endangers minors is strictly prohibited and is removed, the accounts involved are permanently banned, and we report it to the relevant authorities.

1. REBU is for adults only

  • REBU may only be used by people aged 18 and over. Users give their date of birth at sign-up, and accounts under 18 are not allowed.
  • Every host is verified with a live selfie and a government ID before they can go live, take calls or earn, confirming they are an adult.
  • If we learn that a user is under 18, we suspend the account immediately.

2. What is prohibited

  • Any sexual content involving minors, including images, video, live streams, text, drawings or AI-generated material.
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation of or sexualised conversation with a minor, or any attempt to contact minors for sexual purposes.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, or sharing, requesting, selling or linking to CSAM in any form.
  • Any content that sexualises, endangers or exploits children in any other way.

3. How to report

  • In the app: use Report on any profile, chat, call, live stream, room or post. Reports are anonymous and go straight to our moderation team.
  • By email: support@rebu.app with the subject "Child safety report". Include the user's name or ID and what happened, but do not attach or forward any abusive images.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, contact local police first (in India dial 112, or the Childline helpline 1098). In India you can also report to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in.

4. How we act

  • Child safety reports are treated as the highest priority and reviewed as quickly as possible.
  • Violating content is removed, and offending accounts are permanently banned and blocked from signing up again.
  • We preserve relevant evidence and report confirmed CSAM and child exploitation to the appropriate authorities, including Indian law enforcement and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as required by law.
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement requests.
  • Live streams, posts and profiles are moderated, and users can block anyone at any time.

5. Compliance with law

REBU complies with applicable child safety laws, including India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, as well as Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

6. Child safety contact

Our designated contact for child safety matters, including questions about these standards and our CSAM prevention practices: support@rebu.app
