Delete your REBU account

REBU · rebu.live

You can delete your REBU account at any time, either inside the app or by sending us a request.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open REBU and sign in.
  2. Tap your photo at the top left to open Me.
  3. Go to Settings → Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: send us a request

Email support@rebu.app with the subject "Delete my REBU account" from the email linked to your account, or include the mobile number you sign in with.

We may ask you to confirm the request from that number. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, photos, bio, email address and phone number are removed straight away, and the account can no longer be used on any device.
  • Host verification documents (selfie and ID photos) are deleted when you request deletion by email, unless we must keep them for a legal reason, which we will tell you.
  • Any unused coins are lost when the account is deleted. Hosts should withdraw their available earnings first.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Payment, coin and earnings records, for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Messages you already sent stay with the people you sent them to, shown as coming from "Deleted user".
  • Records of reports and safety actions, to prevent abuse and banned users returning.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
